Habit Tracker

How to use

  1. Add up to 10 habits below — type your own, or tap a suggestion.
  2. Click any square in a habit's row to mark that day as done. Click again to undo. Today is the circled date, highlighted down the whole column.
  3. Rename a habit by clicking its name, or remove it with the × at the end of its row.
  4. Everything saves automatically in your browser, so it's still here next time.
